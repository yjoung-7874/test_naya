My Favorite Singersongwriters

5 artists I love: Taylor Swift, Maisie Peters, Sasha Sloan, Conan Gray, and Alec Benjamin.

I love their music, especially the lyrics. They give me comfort and I can relate to them when I'm having a hard time or even when I'm happy. They are the main reason why I want to be a singersongwriter. I want to write songs that comfort people like they do and perform to connect.

Maisie Peters is my second favorite artist.
